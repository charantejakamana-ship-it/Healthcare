# PranaCare Multi-Speciality Hospital — Healthcare Website

A responsive hospital and healthcare webpage built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Included Sections
- **Hospital Information & Hero**: Accreditation badges, bed/specialist statistics, OPD hours, and location.
- **Departments**: 6 clinical departments (Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopaedics, Paediatrics, Oncology, Internal Medicine) linked directly to doctor filtering.
- **Doctor Profiles**: Live search and department filter with qualifications, experience, OPD timings, consultation fees in INR, and one-click appointment pre-fill.
- **Medical Services**: 24/7 Emergency, NABL Diagnostic Lab, 24-Hour Pharmacy, Teleconsultation, Health Checkups, and Cashless Insurance Desk.
- **Appointment Booking & My Bookings**: Interactive booking form with department-synced doctor selection, `localStorage` persistence, and a bookings manager modal, plus a quick BMI Calculator.
- **Health Information**: Patient education articles with a full reader modal.
- **Emergency Contacts**: Top emergency strip and dedicated 24/7 section with Ambulance (108), Trauma Desk, Blood Bank, and National Helplines (112 / 104).

## How to Run
1. Extract `hospital-healthcare-website.zip`.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
